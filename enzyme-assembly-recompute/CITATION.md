## Source dataset

The input tables originate from supplementary Tables S1–S3 associated with the manuscript:

*A Systematic Test of Condensate Predictability and Its Implications for Metabolic Route Selection in Cancer*.

The available provenance record contains the manuscript title, supplement filename, table identifiers, and source checksum. It does not include verified authorship or publication identifiers. Those details must be obtained from the authoritative manuscript record before preparing a complete scholarly reference.

This workflow is an independent reimplementation of the feature-table analysis. It is not the upstream manuscript's complete original software distribution. Data provenance is documented in [docs/data_sources.md](docs/data_sources.md).
